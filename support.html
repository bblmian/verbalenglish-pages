遇到问题先试这三步

  • 把 App 划掉重开一次
  • 「我的 → 权限设置」，确认麦克风（跟读要用）和相机（拍照录入要用）是开着的
  • 「我的 → 第三方服务」，看看「语音识别与朗读」和「整理、翻译与查词」这两项的状态。它们不是必须打开的——不同意也能存段落、能练、能听示范；但跟读识别要第一项，中文翻译、自动标题和查词要第二项

录音与识别

  • 按住麦克风没反应，屏幕上也不出字

    多半是麦克风权限没给，或者「语音识别与朗读」还没同意。前者去「我的 → 权限设置」，后者去「我的 → 第三方服务」。两个都好了还是不行的话，换个安静一点的地方再试 —— 嘈杂环境下识别会很吃力。

  • 提示「没听清，再试一次」

    这句在网络不稳或识别服务临时出错时也会出现，不一定是你读得不好。点提示上的「重试」再来一次；连着几次都这样就等一会儿，或换个网络。

  • 提示「音频还没准备好，再试一次」或「引擎没有响应，再试一次」

    这是还没开始录就失败了 —— 系统没把麦克风交给 App，通话中、刚挂断电话、或者别的 App 正在占用音频时最常见。这跟读得好不好没关系，等几秒再点一次麦克风就行。

  • 提示「录音被打断了」，还有个「再来一次」

    来电、闹钟或别的 App 抢走了音频。本 App 有意不自动接着录（不然你接完电话回来，它已经在偷偷录了），所以要你自己点一下「再来一次」。

  • 读着读着停了，提示「识别结束了，点麦克风继续」

    识别连接断开或到了单次时长上限。已经读到的遍数不会丢，点一下麦克风接着读就行。网络不稳时会比较容易碰到。

  • 提示「语音服务暂时不可用，稍后再试」

    可能是今天用得比较多（每台设备每天有调用上限），也可能是同时用的人多、服务正忙。过一会儿再试。每日额度按北京时间（UTC+8）的零点重置，不是你所在时区的零点。

  • 提示「离线中，联网后再试」

    跟读识别要联网，这条提示说的就是它。离线时其余的照常：可以听示范（换成手机系统自带的声音，不是你选的音色）、可以复习、也可以新增段落（本机断句，只是没有中文翻译和自动标题）。

  • 某一句我明明读完了，进度却不往前走

    本 App 的判定是「读完即过」，故意放宽的：每句允许漏掉少量词，缩写、数字写法不同都算读到。但这个「允许漏」的额度很小（句子越短额度越少），所以短句最容易卡。

    已知会白白吃掉一格额度的情况：句首是一个中文拼音名字（例如 “Daming asked…”，大写小写都一样）。这时候只要其余每个词都读清楚，仍然能过——但不能再漏第二个词了。所以对付卡住的句子，有用的做法是放慢、把每个词都读实，而不是整句重读一遍。

    实在过不去，可以在段落详情页「⋯ → 重置进度」从头再背这一段（会清掉这一段的练习进度）。

  • 它会给我的发音打分吗？

    不会。本 App 只判断你有没有把这句读完，不打分、不纠音、不做语调分析。设计上就是要你多读几遍，而不是读得多标准。

示范朗读

  • 练习页上示范按钮是灰的，按不动

    正在录音时示范按钮会禁用（录音和放音互斥，免得喇叭的声音被麦克风录进去）。先松开录音，按钮就会恢复。

  • 点了示范没有声音

    依次检查手机侧面的静音开关、音量、有没有插着耳机或连着蓝牙设备。

  • 换了示范音色之后，每句都要多等一下，缓存还变大了

    这是正常的：朗读音频按音色分别缓存，换了音色等于全部要重新合成一遍，所以每句第一次会慢一点。旧音色的音频不会自动删掉，还占着空间 —— 介意的话去「我的 → 清除朗读缓存」清一次。

  • 我选的音色自己变回默认了

    服务端下架某个音色时，App 会自动回落到默认音色，免得示范整个失声。去「我的 → 示范音色」重新选一个就行。

  • 没同意「语音识别与朗读」，还能听示范吗？

    能。不同意时示范朗读改用手机系统自带的声音，句子不会发送出去；只是不能录音跟读。

  • 想腾点空间

    「我的 → 清除朗读缓存」。段落和练习进度都不会动，只是把存下来的朗读声音删掉，下次朗读要重新合成、会多等一下。

录入段落

  • 拍照识别出来的文字有错

    识别结果页可以直接改，改完再保存。拍的时候让纸面平整、光线均匀、文字尽量占满画面会准很多。照片只在你的手机上处理，不会上传；不过保存时，识别出来的文字会按你同意的范围发出去做断句和翻译（见下面「我的数据」）。

  • 段落没有中文翻译，或者标题是空的

    保存时没联网、或者没同意「整理、翻译与查词」就会这样（这时用的是本机断句）。补救：打开这一段的详情页，「⋯ → 补上中文」。标题不会自动补 —— 标题本来就随你改，没法判断哪个算「缺」，直接在「⋯ → 编辑正文」里写一个就行。

  • 长按单词查不出意思

    查词要同意「整理、翻译与查词」，并且需要联网。另外每天有次数上限，按北京时间（UTC+8）的零点重置。如果你刚一口气查了很多个词，提示也可能是临时限流 —— 这种等一两分钟再试通常就好了，不用等到第二天。

  • 提示「没保存成功」/「进度没能保存」

    多半是手机存储空间不够。去 iPhone「设置 → 通用 → iPhone 储存空间」腾一点出来再试。这条值得当回事 —— 空间满的时候，一整轮练习的进度可能存不下去。

  • 段落删错了

    「我的 → 回收站」里找得回来。删掉的段落在回收站里保留 30 天，之后自动彻底删除；也可以在回收站里手动彻底删除。

我的数据

  • 我的段落和进度存在哪里？换手机会不会丢？

    都存在这台设备上，目前没有云端备份。换手机或删除 App 之后，这些内容不会自动回来，也无法恢复。

    需要说清楚的是：段落本身不会被上传保存，但在你同意之后，录音和段落文字会为了完成对应功能发送给服务方处理 —— 本 App 的服务器在日本，语音识别、朗读和大模型服务在中国大陆。完整说明见隐私政策。

  • 「第三方服务」的开关我明明同意过，怎么自己关了？

    告知内容有更新时，之前的同意会退回「待确认」、开关也跟着关掉 —— 这是有意的：内容变了就该重新问你一次，而不是拿旧的同意接着用。那一行会写着「告知内容有更新，打开可重新确认」，点开看一眼再决定就行。

  • 怎么把我的数据全部删掉？

    删除 App 即可清除本机的段落、进度和缓存。例外：一个随机生成的设备码存在系统钥匙串里，重装后仍然存在。它是一串随机字符，不包含任何和你本人有关的信息，只用来区分设备和限制每天的调用次数；App 内目前没有清除它的入口。

    如果要删服务器上与这个设备码相关的记录，发邮件给我并附上设备码 —— 在「我的」页最下方点「复制」拿到完整的码（页面上显示的只有前 8 位）。用「给作者写信」也行，但设备码要自己粘进去（那封信里只带版本、机型、系统）——复制后 2 分钟内粘贴有效，过时了再点一次「复制」。提前说明：服务器上只有每天的调用次数计数，而它每天自动清零覆盖，所以你写信过来时多半已经不存在了；我会确认并回复你。

  • 我不想让内容发给第三方 AI，可以吗？

    可以。「我的 → 第三方服务」里把开关关掉，之后不再发送该类数据。关掉之后：段落照样能存能练（本机断句、没有中文翻译和自动标题），示范朗读改用系统声音，查词不可用，段落的小图标换成统一的分类图案，首页显示固定的问候语。完整说明见隐私政策。

Note: the app's interface is in Chinese only, so every menu path below is given in English with the exact on-screen Chinese in brackets — look for the bracketed text on your device.

Three things to try first

  • Force-quit the app and open it again
  • Me (我的) → Permissions (权限设置): make sure Microphone (needed to read along) and Camera (needed to capture text from a photo) are allowed
  • Me (我的) → Third-party services (第三方服务): check the two entries “语音识别与朗读” (speech recognition & read-aloud) and “整理、翻译与查词” (tidy-up, translation & word lookup). They are not required — without them you can still save paragraphs, practise, and hear the demo; but reading along needs the first, and Chinese translation, auto titles and word lookup need the second

Recording and recognition

  • I hold the microphone button and nothing happens — no text appears

    Usually microphone permission is missing, or “语音识别与朗读” hasn't been agreed to. For the first, go to Me (我的) → Permissions (权限设置); for the second, Me (我的) → Third-party services (第三方服务). If both are fine and it still fails, try somewhere quieter — recognition struggles in noisy places.

  • “没听清，再试一次” (Didn't catch that, try again)

    This also appears when the network is flaky or the recognition service has a temporary error — it isn't necessarily about how you read. Tap “重试” (Retry) on the message. If it happens several times in a row, wait a moment or switch networks.

  • “音频还没准备好，再试一次” (Audio isn't ready) or “引擎没有响应，再试一次” (Engine didn't respond)

    These mean recording never started — the system didn't hand the microphone over. Most common during or just after a phone call, or when another app is holding the audio session. Nothing to do with your reading; wait a few seconds and tap the microphone again.

  • “录音被打断了” (Recording was interrupted), with a “再来一次” (Try again) button

    A call, an alarm or another app took the audio session. The app deliberately does not resume on its own (otherwise it would quietly be recording again when you come back from a call), so tap “再来一次” yourself.

  • It stops mid-practice with “识别结束了，点麦克风继续” (Recognition ended — tap the mic to continue)

    The recognition connection dropped, or a single session hit its length limit. The repetitions you already completed are not lost — just tap the microphone and carry on. More likely on an unstable network.

  • “语音服务暂时不可用，稍后再试” (Speech service temporarily unavailable)

    Either you've used a lot today (each device has a daily call limit), or the service is busy with many users at once. Try again a little later. The daily allowance resets at midnight Beijing time (UTC+8), not midnight in your own time zone.

  • “离线中，联网后再试” (Offline — try again when connected)

    Reading along needs a connection, and that is what this message is about. Everything else still works offline: you can hear the demo (it switches to your iPhone's built-in system voice rather than the voice you chose), review, and add paragraphs (split on the device, just without a Chinese translation or an auto-generated title).

  • I finished the sentence but the progress doesn't move on

    The rule is “read it through and it counts”, and it is deliberately lenient: a small number of missed words per sentence is allowed, and contractions or different ways of writing numbers still count. That allowance is small, though, and shorter sentences get less of it — so short sentences are the ones that stick.

    One case is known to use up that allowance for nothing: a sentence starting with a Chinese pinyin given name (e.g. “Daming asked…”, in any capitalisation). You can still get through it — as long as every other word is clear, because you can't afford to miss a second one. So for a stuck sentence the thing that actually helps is to slow down and pronounce every word fully, not to re-read the whole sentence.

    If it really won't pass, the paragraph detail page has ⋯ → Reset progress (重置进度), which starts that paragraph over (and clears its practice progress).

  • Does it score my pronunciation?

    No. The app only checks whether you got through the sentence. There is no scoring, no pronunciation correction and no intonation analysis — the point is to repeat a passage many times, not to hit a standard.

Demo read-aloud

  • The demo button on the practice screen is greyed out

    It is disabled while you are recording (recording and playback are mutually exclusive, so the speaker isn't picked up by the microphone). Release the record button and it comes back.

  • I tap the demo button and hear nothing

    Check the silent switch on the side of your iPhone, the volume, and whether headphones or a Bluetooth device are connected.

  • After changing the demo voice, every sentence takes longer and the cache got bigger

    That's expected: read-aloud audio is cached per voice, so changing voice means everything has to be synthesised again — the first play of each sentence is slower. The old voice's audio is not deleted automatically and still takes up space; clear it with Me (我的) → Clear read-aloud cache (清除朗读缓存).

  • The voice I picked reverted to the default

    When a voice is withdrawn on the server the app falls back to the default rather than losing the demo entirely. Pick another one in Me (我的) → Demo voice (示范音色).

  • Can I still hear the demo without agreeing to “语音识别与朗读”?

    Yes. Without consent the demo uses your iPhone's built-in system voice and no sentence is sent anywhere; you just can't record and read along.

  • I want to free up some space

    Me (我的) → Clear read-aloud cache (清除朗读缓存). Your paragraphs and progress are untouched — it only deletes the stored audio, so the next read-aloud has to be synthesised again and takes a moment longer.

Adding paragraphs

  • The text recognised from my photo has mistakes

    You can edit it directly on the results screen before saving. A flat page, even lighting, and filling the frame with the text all help a lot. Photos are processed on your device only and are never uploaded — but when you save, the recognised text is sent out for sentence splitting and translation, within the consent you've given (see “Your data”).

  • A paragraph has no Chinese translation, or no title

    That happens when it was saved offline, or without consent for “整理、翻译与查词” (sentence splitting then runs on the device). To fix it, open that paragraph's detail page and choose ⋯ → Fill in Chinese (补上中文). Titles are not backfilled — a title is yours to edit, so there's no way to tell which ones are “missing”. Just write one in ⋯ → Edit text (编辑正文).

  • Long-pressing a word doesn't look it up

    Word lookup needs consent for “整理、翻译与查词” and a network connection. There is also a daily limit, which resets at midnight Beijing time (UTC+8). If you have just looked up a lot of words in quick succession, the same message can also mean short-term rate limiting — in that case a minute or two is usually enough, no need to wait for tomorrow.

  • “没保存成功” (Couldn't save) or “进度没能保存” (Progress couldn't be saved)

    Most often your iPhone is out of storage. Free some space in Settings → General → iPhone Storage and try again. Worth taking seriously — when storage is full, a whole round of practice can fail to save.

  • I deleted a paragraph by mistake

    Look in Me (我的) → Recycle bin (回收站). Deleted paragraphs stay there for 30 days and are then removed permanently; you can also remove them permanently yourself from the recycle bin.

Your data

  • Where are my paragraphs and progress stored? Will I lose them on a new phone?

    Everything is stored on this device, and there is no cloud backup at the moment. After switching phones or deleting the app, that content does not come back and cannot be recovered.

    To be precise: paragraphs are not uploaded for storage, but once you consent, your recordings and paragraph text are sent to service providers so those features can work — this app's own server is in Japan, and the speech recognition, read-aloud and large-language-model services are in mainland China. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

  • I agreed to the third-party services — why did the switches turn themselves off?

    When the disclosure text is updated, your earlier consent goes back to “undecided” and the switch turns off with it. That is deliberate: if what you were told has changed, you should be asked again rather than have the old consent carried over. The row says “告知内容有更新，打开可重新确认” (the disclosure has been updated — open to confirm again); open it, read it, and decide.

  • How do I delete all of my data?

    Deleting the app removes the paragraphs, progress and caches on your device. One exception: a randomly generated device code is stored in the system keychain and survives a reinstall. It is a random string that contains nothing about you personally, used only to tell devices apart and to cap daily calls; there is currently no in-app way to clear it.

    To have the server-side records for that device code deleted, email me and include the code — tap “复制” (Copy) at the bottom of the Me (我的) page to get the full code (only the first 8 characters are shown on screen). Writing via Write to the author (给作者写信) works too, but you have to paste the device code in yourself (that draft carries only the version, device model and OS) — the copy stays on the clipboard for 2 minutes, so tap “复制” again if it has expired. Up front: the server holds nothing but per-day call counters, and those reset and overwrite every day, so by the time you write there is usually nothing left; I'll confirm and reply either way.

  • Can I use the app without sending anything to third-party AI services?

    Yes. Switch them off in Me (我的) → Third-party services (第三方服务) and no further data of that kind is sent. With them off: paragraphs can still be saved and practised (split on the device, without a Chinese translation or auto title), the demo uses the system voice, word lookup is unavailable, paragraph icons fall back to a shared category symbol, and the home screen shows a fixed greeting. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
